## Limitations and holds

- **FAIL hold: the applied-geometry contract is not met for 4K.** The
  original #546 symptom (fit sized to the union of both heads) is gone — the
  requested rectangle is single-monitor sized and contained every round — but
  the compositor-applied window hangs 103 px past the monitor's right edge,
  deterministically, because Mutter keeps the initial placement x while the
  app can only resize (never move) on Wayland. This is exactly the failure
  class the request-only check could not see; it needs its own app-side fix
  (constrain the fit size/anchor to what the current position allows, or
  re-fit on the post-configure geometry).
- Headless-only evidence: no live-desktop run, no packaged candidate, no
  pointer input. The remembered-geometry path beyond three re-opens per clip
  is not covered.
- 4K round 1 needed two relaunches before a fit decision (the known #627
  startup-crash class, out of scope here; dead-launch logs kept as
  `fit-4k-1.log.attempt{1,2}` in the run root, not part of the committed
  evidence set).
- `dbus.pid`, `setscales.log`, and `shell.log` are session plumbing and were
  not committed; the committed set is the fit evidence plus `layout.txt` and
